About

robyn wong min xuan (she/her) is a butoh and experimental theatre artist based between Singapore and New York. she often finds herself taking “ordinary” things and wringing out their familiarity, taking play quite seriously, and blurring the lines between observer/observed. she looks forward to witnessing you witness her.
